How this was made

Every livestream from the past year was taken from each channel's streams tab and stream playlists (including unlisted VODs). Their chat replays were downloaded and every superchat, super sticker and gifted membership read out. 102 streams have no chat data: YouTube keeps no chat replay for them (usually because the VOD was edited after the stream, or chat was off), or they are members-only or age-restricted.

People are matched by YouTube channel ID, so someone who renamed their account still counts once. Superchats sent in other currencies were converted to US dollars at the exchange rates of 2026-11-12, not the rate on the day they were sent; about 6% of the money came in other currencies.

Gifted memberships have no price in the chat. A gift is always the channel's cheapest membership level, so they are valued at that level's US price (Pisco $4.99, Counterpoints $2.99, The Soy Pill $2.99, Lib & Learn $1.99). That makes them an estimate, so they are kept out of the dollar totals above. Memberships a channel gifted from its own account are left out. Regular memberships aren't counted at all.

The figures are what viewers paid; YouTube keeps about 30%. Only public chat is used.
